## About the project

The series of illustrations, published during my senior year, is based on my personal perspective on contemporary cities and serves as a graphic form of urban planning. The built environment today shows an elastic character — pop-up stores, temporary pavilions and demountable structures — and these characteristics are likely to shape the building technology of the future.
