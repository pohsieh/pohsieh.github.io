## About the project

The focus of this project is not only the building but the semi-outdoor space. Its core is urban regeneration through a small-scale theater that includes a café, retail stores and a black-box auditorium. To connect the main street and the inner courtyard, a sunken plaza is placed under the volume of the theater. With the black-box theater floating above, the sunken space becomes a transparent outdoor theater where people relax and interact. The winding circulation offers many perspectives from which to appreciate the old village at a slow pace.
