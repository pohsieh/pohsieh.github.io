## About the project

Wholesale markets, from which most foods in the city are distributed, are an urban-scale machine. However, traditional wholesale markets often operate secretly inside a solid volume. The project proposes a transparent form of market by adding a public circulation system and reorganizing the freight logistics system. The design was generated with a part-to-whole method, which creates a scenario-based relation between people and places.
