## About the project

As urbanization expands, the demand for collective housing has increased. To generate a new form of future living unit, computational design thinking and digital manufacturing tools were applied. Each unit of the nest is made of vertical and horizontal wood components that also serve as the structure. Through computational simulation and fabrication, the project focuses on the possibilities of structural form and material science.
