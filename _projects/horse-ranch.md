## About the project

The riverside on the edge of the city used to be a horse ranch, but it became an abandoned urban space after the industry collapsed. To revitalize the area, the project constructs a lightweight pavilion in which the horse ranch is reintroduced as both a public retreat and a contemporary landscape.
