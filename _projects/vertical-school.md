## About the project

Design institutions today emphasize interdisciplinary collaboration, which often brings out fascinating results. To encourage creative cooperation, the design creates an illuminated atrium between the studios, so that students from different backgrounds easily meet and more interaction and communication can happen. The idea of a canvas relates to the many design ideas that may appear in the atrium. The design also brings the creative process to the public through the transparency of the space: a semi-outdoor foyer, louvers, staircases and translucent glazing.
